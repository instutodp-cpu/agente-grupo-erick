# Hermes Legal Source Registry v1

Status: J02 / metadata-only / no provider execution.

## Principle
A source is not evidence merely because it was retrieved. Every source has identity, authority class, jurisdiction, temporal scope, usage constraints and immutable snapshot provenance.

## Initial source classes
- planalto: primary official federal legislation.
- lexml: official legal information network / identifiers and metadata.
- cnj_datajud: official process metadata; not presumed complete or current.
- stf: official constitutional court material.
- stj: official superior court material.
- tst: official superior labor court material.
- anpd: official data-protection authority material.
- tribunal_official: other official court sources.
- internal: organization-controlled documents.
- open_web: discovery only until independently verified.

J02 registers source policy only. It contains no credentials, network adapter or live fetch.
