# Content Production — C04

## Purpose
Transform an approved creative direction into production-ready structured content while preserving evidence, brand constraints and unknown commercial facts.

## Flow
creative brief -> format blueprint -> copy/script -> asset requirements -> CTA -> factual QA -> production package

## Rules
- Do not invent price, discount, stock, margin, product specification, testimonial or urgency.
- Preserve evidence references and hypotheses from C03.
- Every deliverable must state its commercial objective and CTA.
- Copy must fit the selected format rather than merely reformatting the same text.
- Reel scripts separate spoken line, on-screen text and visual direction.
- Stories are sequenced frames with one job per frame.
- Carousels are sequenced slides with a coherent progression.
- Unknown required facts become placeholders/blockers, never fabricated copy.
- C04 produces drafts/packages only. No rendering, publishing, messaging or ad mutation.
