# Research & Intelligence — C02

## Purpose
Turn missing decision information into traceable evidence before strategy or creative work.

## Research trigger
Research is required when a recommendation depends on current trends, competitors, active offers, current platform behavior, current market references, or other externally changing facts.

Research is not required merely to restate immutable internal facts already supported by trusted evidence.

## Flow
objective -> decision questions -> evidence gaps -> source plan -> collect -> normalize -> assess -> synthesize -> recommendation

## Rules
- Never label content, format or topic "viral" without current supporting evidence.
- Never present memory as current external state.
- Preserve source URL/reference, retrieval time, observed claim and scope.
- Separate source fact, observation, inference and hypothesis.
- Prefer primary/official sources for platform/API facts.
- Conflicting evidence must be surfaced, not silently discarded.
- Insufficient evidence returns INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, not fabricated certainty.
- C02 is read-only. No publishing, messaging, ad mutation or destructive action.
