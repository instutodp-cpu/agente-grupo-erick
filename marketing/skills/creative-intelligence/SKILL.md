# Creative Intelligence — C03

## Purpose
Convert evidence-backed marketing opportunities into testable creative concepts without publishing or rendering them.

## Flow
commercial objective -> audience/context -> evidence -> creative hypothesis -> format -> hook -> structure -> CTA -> variants -> QA hypothesis

## Rules
- Commercial outcome precedes vanity metrics.
- Every material creative choice must reference evidence, an internal constraint, or be explicitly labeled as a hypothesis.
- Never promise virality or sales.
- Never copy competitor creative verbatim; extract patterns and transform them for Erick context.
- Separate proven brand rules from experimental creative hypotheses.
- Generate meaningfully different variants, not superficial synonym swaps.
- Missing price, stock, margin, product claim or offer data must remain unknown; never invent it.
- C03 is simulation/draft only: no rendering, publishing, messaging or ad mutation.
