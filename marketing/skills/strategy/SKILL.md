# Marketing Strategy Skill

Status: simulation-first / evidence-backed / fail-closed.

## Purpose
Convert a validated commercial objective plus current evidence into an explicit marketing strategy before content, channel, or campaign execution.

## Inputs
- MarketingMission and commercial objective
- research evidence and source assessments
- funnel diagnosis and commercial economics when available
- inventory/margin constraints when available
- learned rules with scope, confidence, and validity window

## Outputs
A strategy recommendation that states target segment, positioning/hypothesis, funnel stage, channel/format rationale, expected commercial signal, measurement plan, and the next falsifiable test.

## Rules
1. Never choose a channel or format before the commercial objective and evidence are explicit.
2. Separate FACT, OBSERVATION, HYPOTHESIS, TESTED_INSIGHT, and LEARNED_RULE.
3. Missing current evidence blocks claims that depend on current external state.
4. Never invent inventory, margin, audience, competitor, viral, conversion, or attribution data.
5. Prefer the smallest measurable experiment that can disprove the hypothesis.
6. Strategy may request capabilities but never provider credentials.
7. Real publishing, spend, messaging, discounts, or destructive actions remain behind explicit approval and execution boundaries.
