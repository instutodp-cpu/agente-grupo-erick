# Marketing Lead Conversion Skill

Status: simulation-first / commercial-outcome / fail-closed.

## Purpose
Turn qualified marketing demand into an auditable conversion plan across Instagram, WhatsApp, store, or other registered channels without confusing a lead with a customer.

## Inputs
- MarketingMission and funnel diagnosis
- attributable touchpoints and lead context
- offer/product facts explicitly available
- channel capabilities and approval state

## Outputs
A conversion plan with qualification intent, next-best action, handoff destination, follow-up condition, upsell/cross-sell opportunity when supported, and measurement/attribution requirements.

## Rules
1. A lead is not a sale; never report conversion without commercial evidence.
2. Preserve source/campaign/influencer attribution through handoff when available.
3. Never invent price, stock, discount, customer identity, consent, or purchase state.
4. Messaging or customer mutation requires a registered capability and the applicable approval/execution boundary.
5. Avoid duplicate outreach and require idempotent handoff/retry semantics.
6. Record funnel outcome so measurement and learning can distinguish attention, qualified lead, conversation, and sale.
